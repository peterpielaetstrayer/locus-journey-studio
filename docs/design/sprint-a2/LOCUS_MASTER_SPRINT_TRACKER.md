## North-star outcome

A coherent, field-ready prototype in which:

- the learner experience feels unmistakably LOCUS;
- the creator authors learning into real places;
- the orchestrator perceives learners through evidence and judgment;
- the reviewer can inspect quality, safety, and accessibility;
- the connected loop is credible without pretending to be production-ready.

## Operating rules

1. Do not expand active scope mid-sprint.
2. Do not call a screen finished because it compiles.
3. Every screen requires browser review at desktop and mobile widths.
4. Every sprint requires at least one refinement pass.
5. Preserve working data, learning logic, routes, safety, and accessibility.
6. Use a real visual target before implementation.
7. Keep new ideas in the parking lot instead of interrupting current execution.
8. Freeze completed screens unless a later shared-system change creates a real regression.

---

## Definition of sprint completion

Sprint A2 is complete only when:

- all four screens are implemented;
- existing learner state and deterministic adaptation still work;
- each screen matches the established Golden Screen 1 quality;
- desktop and mobile compositions are intentional;
- no screen is primarily a generic card stack or form;
- tests, typecheck, lint, and build pass;
- keyboard and reduced-motion behavior are verified;
- all four screens are reviewed in the browser;
- one focused refinement pass is completed;
- remaining limitations and asset gaps are documented.

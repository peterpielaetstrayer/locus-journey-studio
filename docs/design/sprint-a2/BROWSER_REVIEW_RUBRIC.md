## Review method

For each screen:

1. Look for ten seconds without interacting.
2. State what your eye notices first.
3. Complete the primary action.
4. Test the important state change.
5. Repeat on mobile.
6. Test keyboard-only.
7. Test reduced motion.
8. Record at least three issues.
9. Refine.
10. review again.

---

# Screen-level questions

## Journey Awakening

- Does it feel like a new mystery after the entrance, or a second homepage?
- Is only part of the route visible?
- Is the central question dominant?
- Is the atlas preview intriguing but secondary?
- Does baseline capture feel collected rather than administrative?
- Is “Begin walking” the unmistakable primary action?
- Are route and accessibility details discoverable?

## Water Fingerprint

- Does the environment dominate before capture controls?
- Do capture modes feel like tools rather than form inputs?
- Is the learner asked one good question at a time?
- Does evidence visibly change the route?
- Does the saved note feel owned by the learner?
- Is confidence understandable without the number becoming the point?
- Does the success state avoid gamification?

## Cypress-Knee Mystery

- Do the cypress knees feel visually unavoidable?
- Is the learner invited to theorize before being taught?
- Is the theory visibly provisional?
- Is the adaptive question quiet and exact?
- Are evidence and theory clearly distinct?
- Is safety visible without overwhelming the mystery?
- Does the screen avoid quiz styling?

## Living Atlas

- Does it look like an authored object rather than a dashboard?
- Is learner evidence visibly central?
- Are original and revised thinking distinguishable?
- Is the systems map legible?
- Does unresolved uncertainty remain visible?
- Does the artifact feel printable?
- Does mobile preserve the feeling of one continuous atlas object?

---

# Cross-screen consistency

- Typography matches Golden Screen 1.
- Dark environmental surfaces share one grade.
- Amber is sparse and meaningful.
- Parchment is reserved for collected evidence and artifacts.
- Buttons share the expedition-action language.
- Studio access remains secondary.
- Prototype and simulated-AI disclosures remain legible.
- Route thread grows coherently through the journey.
- Screens do not revert to generic cards.
- Motion is quiet and purposeful.
